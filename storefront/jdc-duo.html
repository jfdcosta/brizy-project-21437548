JDC DUO / WORKING PERSONAL PROTOTYPE

Two watches.
One place.

JDC Duo is a matching pair of 3D printed docks for an IKEA SKÅDIS pegboard. Mirrored side openings make room for a closed watch bracelet; removable inserts hold the original Pixel Watch 3 (45 mm) and Galaxy Watch4 (44 mm) charging pucks.

Prototype — not available to orderPrice and release date have not been set.

WHAT HAS BEEN CHECKED

Made and fitted.

The pair was printed in PLA and mounted on the creator’s SKÅDIS board. The Pixel insert fitted; a revised Galaxy insert later held its charger in a physical fit check.

BEFORE A RELEASE

Still being tested.

Long term charging, heat, load and durability checks are unfinished. Fit can vary with charger revision and print tolerance. The watches, charging pucks, cables and pegboard shown in the photos are not part of the dock.

Independent prototype; not an IKEA, Google or Samsung product.
